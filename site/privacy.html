Open Handover

Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026

What Handover is

Handover is a Google Apps Script web app. When you open it, it runs under your own Google account with the permissions you grant on Google’s consent screen.

What it accesses

Handover asks for access to your Google Drive so it can list your folders and files, show their sizes and your storage use, and create or accept ownership transfer requests that you choose. It also reads your email address to show which account you’re signed in with.

What it stores

Nothing. Handover has no database and no server of its own. File names, sizes and contents stay inside Google. The developer can’t see your files or your account.

What it changes

Only what you ask for: it adds the receiving account as an editor with a pending ownership request on the files you pick, and, in the receiving account, accepts those requests and files loose items into a folder named after the sender.

Removing access

You can remove Handover’s access at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps with account access.

Use of Google user data

Handover’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Handover is free and independent. It isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google. Google Drive and Gmail are trademarks of Google LLC.
